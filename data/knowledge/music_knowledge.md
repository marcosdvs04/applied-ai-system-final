# VibeMatch Knowledge Base

Each `##` section is one retrievable chunk. Lines such as `keywords:`, `genre:`, `mood:`,
`energy:` and `acoustic:` are metadata the system uses to build a listener profile.
The `note:` line is the human-readable explanation that gets cited in answers.
Genre and mood values must exist in data/songs.csv.
